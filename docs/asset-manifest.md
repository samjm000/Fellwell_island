# Asset manifest

Every major asset, where it lives, and where it came from. **Status:**
**runtime** means loaded by the game; **source** means an original that runtime
assets are made from; **concept** means design or reference only; **deprecated**
means superseded and kept only for comparison; **prototype** means
experimental and not used by the game.

The animation frames have a machine-readable record too:
`art/characters/stella/animations/manifest.json` (source sheet SHA-256, frames,
fps, scale, anchors). `tests/test_asset_manifest.gd` checks it.

## Canonical designs

> **Canonical Stella:** brown, wavy, shoulder-length hair with short bangs;
> large brown eyes; yellow-and-white striped T-shirt with multicoloured sleeve
> trim; denim shorts; white socks; pink high-top trainers; small beaded
> necklace. Source of truth: `assets_source/concept_art/stella_lemon_character_sheet.png`,
> as drawn in the animation sheets in `assets_source/character_sheets/stella/`.
>
> **Canonical Lemon:** a small, soft, fluffy toy dog with dark eyes, a blue
> collar and a yellow LEMON tag. The latest direction is soft golden; the
> current sheets draw her cream (flagged below).

## Open art issues

- **Lighthouse:** the story has the lighthouse derelict, but the accepted
  garden (night) and bedroom/telescope paintings show it lit, with a beam. The
  paintings are installed as supplied and nothing in the game treats the
  lighthouse as working. Needs an art decision.
- **No cut-out foreground layers for the house.** The hall, kitchen and garden
  paintings are flat, so their painted foreground plants, armchair and walls
  can't pass in front of Stella; the walkable floors keep her clear of them.
- **The video's Stella differs from the canonical design:** red low-top sneakers
  (not pink high-tops with hearts), a shorter bob, and a flatter cartoon style
  than the room paintings. Lemon is cream, with no visible LEMON tag.
- **Idle faces the front; the walks are a three-quarter view.** She turns
  instantly when starting or stopping. The video has the turns (frames 49–60
  and 205–228) if transitions are wanted.
- **Pace:** at her game speed (240 px/s) she steps about 1.9× as fast as in the
  video. Lowering `Stella.walk_speed` towards 130–170 gives the video's pace.
- The pickup is long (3 s at 16 fps).
